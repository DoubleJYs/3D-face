# Data, portrait, asset, and weight access boundary

No data, portrait, biometric tensor, pretrained weight, author-trained
checkpoint, or Mcanon image is redistributed.

Users must independently obtain FFHQ, FaceScape, REALY, DECA, FLAME, SMIRK,
baseline implementations, and evaluator assets from their official providers
and comply with the applicable terms. The authors do not provide a public or
controlled checkpoint-request route in this public release.
