# Public release scope

FaceUV-Eval publishes the author-controlled material needed to inspect the
evaluation and residual-adaptation interfaces and to repeat synthetic and
anonymous statistical checks.

## Included

- Author-created Python source for evaluation, matched controls, post-processing,
  CanonReg, and statistical procedures.
- Deterministic synthetic fixtures and tests that use no real face image,
  dataset, weight, or checkpoint.
- Anonymous derived statistics and robustness summaries.
- Non-face audit figures, their source data, and reconstruction scripts.
- Environment fingerprints, acquisition boundaries, and public provenance
  hashes.

## Not redistributed

- Manuscript and submission files or editorial correspondence.
- Internal work records, cloud execution logs, and permission documents.
- Face images, biometric tensors, identity mappings, or private pair-level data.
- Datasets, pretrained assets, canonical-mask bytes, model weights, or
  checkpoints.
- Local environments, caches, archives, credentials, or machine-specific paths.

Public verification proves the integrity, membership, rights classification,
and documented synthetic or anonymous checks of this release. It does not
substitute for provider licenses and does not reproduce private-data empirical
execution without the separately licensed assets.
