# Reproducibility scope

This is a source-and-synthetic package, not a public end-to-end result package.

Included:

- exact V15 matched-control and CanonReg source packages;
- their minimal local Python dependency closure;
- contracts, requirements, source locks, and synthetic tests;
- deterministic package construction and verification tools;
- Mcanon acquisition and byte/parse verification instructions.

Excluded:

- FFHQ, FaceScape, REALY, and every original or derived biometric tensor;
- source portraits, qualitative images, and case-level rosters;
- B-lite, expression, Full, NoCond, B-lite-FT, and CanonReg weights;
- DECA/FLAME/SMIRK and evaluator assets, including Mcanon;
- manuscript text, tables, figures, PDFs, and private rights evidence;
- formal attempt outputs and the anonymous statistics companion.

Passing package checks proves integrity and synthetic executability only. It
does not prove empirical-result reproduction, new CUDA execution, cross-device
equivalence, publication, or submission readiness.
